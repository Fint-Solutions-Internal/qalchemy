About FINT

FINT builds and ships technology that demands relentless quality, where every release, every integration, every edge case matters. Our QA practice isn't an afterthought; it's a discipline we invest in, because the teams that test well are the teams that ship with confidence.

QAlchemy Expo is our way of turning that discipline outward: a space built by QAs, for QAs, to trade ideas, tools, and hard-won lessons.

About the Expo

QAs are no longer the last checkpoint before release. We're active competitors in the market that rewards speed and quality. QAlchemy Expo is FINT's answer: a full day of real talks on AI in QA, security testing, infrastructure, and the tools we build in-house, capped off with hands-on time in the workshop.

No fluff, no vendor pitches. Just practitioners talking to practitioners.

Before you register

Frequently asked questions

Everything you'd want to know before showing up.

Do I have to pay to attend?

No. QAlchemy Expo is a free FINT QA community event, lunch and snacks included.

I'm not from a QA background, can I attend?

Yes, please do. The sessions cover AI, security, and infra topics that are useful well beyond QA, and the workshop is open to anyone curious enough to try it.

Do I need to bring anything?

Just yourself for the talks. If you're joining the hands-on MobileWright workshop, bring a laptop with the pre-requisites the organizers share closer to the date.

How is this year's event being run?

In person, at the FINT Dallas Center Office in Hyderabad. This is an on-site connect, so plan to be there for the day.

Do I need to register in advance?

Yes. Seats are limited, so grab yours with the "Reserve Your Seat" button above before they fill up.

Is food provided?

Yes, lunch and snacks are provided for everyone registered. No need to bring or arrange your own.

Can I attend just part of the day?

You're welcome to, but the sessions build on each other and the workshop needs its full slot, so we'd recommend blocking out the whole day if you can.

Who do I contact with other questions?

Reach out to the organizing team at qalchemy@fintinc.com and we'll get back to you.
